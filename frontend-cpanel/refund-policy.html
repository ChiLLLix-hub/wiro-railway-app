Refund Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Overview

Mygenx Studio provides AI generation services (images, video, music, 3D and chat) paid for with prepaid account credits. Payments are processed securely by CHIP in Malaysian Ringgit (RM). By purchasing credits you agree to this Refund Policy.

2. Credits and Generations

Credits are held for the estimated cost when you start a generation and are settled when the task finishes. If a generation fails or does not complete, the held credits are returned to your account automatically. If the final cost is lower than the amount held, the difference is returned to your credit balance.

3. Non-Refundable Items

  • Credits consumed by successfully completed generations.
  • Dissatisfaction with the style, quality or content of AI output, as results are probabilistic and vary by prompt and model.
  • Credits lost through account sharing, compromised passwords, or violation of our terms.
  • Accounts suspended or terminated for abuse or unlawful use.
  • Promotional or bonus credits.

4. Refund of Unused Credits

You may request a refund of unused, purchased credits within 7 days of the purchase date, provided that none of the credits from that purchase have been used. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method via CHIP, less any non-recoverable payment gateway fees where applicable.

5. Duplicate or Erroneous Charges

If you were charged more than once, or charged but credits were not added to your account, contact us with your payment reference. Verified duplicate or failed-to-credit payments will be corrected by crediting your account or refunding in full to your original payment method.

6. How to Request a Refund

Email ask@agromar.com.my from your registered email address with your account email, payment reference and reason. We respond within 3 business days.

7. Processing Time

Approved refunds are initiated within 7 business days. Depending on your bank or card issuer, funds may take a further 7 to 14 business days to appear.

8. Chargebacks

Please contact us before filing a chargeback so we can resolve the issue promptly. Accounts with fraudulent chargebacks may be suspended.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always published on this page.

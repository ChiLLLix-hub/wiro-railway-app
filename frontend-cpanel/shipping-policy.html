Shipping Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Digital Service Only

Mygenx Studio sells digital services only: prepaid account credits and AI-generated content. We do not sell or ship physical goods, so no shipping fees or courier delivery apply.

2. Delivery of Credits

After a successful payment through CHIP, credits are added to your account automatically, usually within a few minutes. You will be redirected to a payment confirmation page and can view your balance on your dashboard.

3. Delivery of Generated Content

Generated images, videos, audio, 3D files and text are delivered online in the studio once the task completes. Processing time depends on the model and queue and may range from seconds to several minutes. Download your outputs promptly as storage of outputs may be limited.

4. Delayed or Missing Delivery

If payment was successful but credits do not appear within 1 hour, or a generation does not complete, email ask@agromar.com.my with your payment reference. Failed generations are refunded in credits automatically; see our Refund Policy.

5. Availability

Services are available wherever internet access and our payment methods are supported, subject to applicable law. We may temporarily suspend the service for maintenance.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Introduction

Mygenx Studio ("we", "us"), operated by Agromar in Malaysia, respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect your personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) of Malaysia.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account data: name, email address and hashed password.
  • Usage data: prompts, uploaded reference files, generated outputs, selected models, task status, credit balance and transaction history.
  • Payment data: payment reference, amount and status. Card and bank details are entered on CHIP's payment page and are never stored on our servers.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser/device information, session identifiers and logs for security and fraud prevention.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate and maintain the service and your account.
  • To process payments, credits and refunds.
  • To send verification, security, payment and service emails.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorised access.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We share only the data necessary with: CHIP (payment processing); Wiro AI and underlying model providers (your prompts and uploaded files are sent to generate outputs); and email/SMTP and hosting providers. These providers process data under their own privacy terms. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local/session storage and session identifiers to keep you signed in and to enforce one active session per account. We do not use them for third-party advertising.

6. Data Retention

We keep account and transaction records while your account is active and as required by law (including accounting and tax rules, typically up to 7 years). Generation history may be deleted on request unless needed for legal or fraud-prevention purposes.

7. Security

We use hashed passwords, token-based authentication, HTTPS and access controls. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

8. Your Rights

Under the PDPA you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and withdraw consent, by emailing ask@agromar.com.my. We respond within 21 days. Withdrawing consent may prevent us from providing the service.

9. Children

The service is not intended for persons under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. International Transfers

Some providers are located outside Malaysia. By using the service you consent to such transfers, and we take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate protection.

11. User Content

You are responsible for the prompts and files you submit. Do not submit unlawful content or personal data of others without authorisation.

12. Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version is published here with its revision date.
